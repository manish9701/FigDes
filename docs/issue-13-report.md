### L05 — The active nav item's label takes the same fill as its own background

**Requirement.** A navItem with state 'active' paints its pill in the accent fill and must paint its label in a colour that reads against that fill.

**Known issue.** Label and pill are the same colour, so the active item's label is invisible.

Measured on the applied frame: node 317:420 (the active pill) and 317:421 (its label) render as the same yellow. Rendering each node in isolation shows the glyphs in the accent fill on a white canvas, and the pill as solid accent. Contrast 1.0:1 against a 4.5:1 requirement.

The #14 fix made nav labels follow their surface via onDarkSurface(), which is right for the three inactive items on the dark rail and wrong for the active one: the active item's surface is the accent pill, not the rail. The rule keys on the rail, not on the node the label actually sits in.

review_design reported no text-contrast finding on the 57-node frame that contains this. Either the rule resolves the wrong ancestor for a nav label, or it skips labels whose parent is a component instance. Either way a 1.0:1 pair is being reported as clean, which is the more serious of the two halves.

**Measured.** Active nav label fill equals the active pill fill (contrast 1.0:1); review_design reports 0 text-contrast findings on the frame that contains it.

### L06 — A region with width 'fill' ignores the sibling occupying the left edge

**Requirement.** A region declared { width: 'fill', right: <inspector width> } must be laid out in the space left after the other regions, not from x=0.

**Known issue.** Topology is placed at x=0 with width 1120, while the 220px nav rail occupies x=0..220. The region is therefore underneath the rail, and its contents are occluded.

The compiler's own trace agrees with itself here — this is not the verifier inventing a position. compile_ir emits setPosition x=0 and setSize width=1120 for node 317:418, and the applied frame matches the emitted geometry exactly. The fill width is canvasWidth - right, with no accounting for a region already placed at the left edge.

The render shows all three device nodes (mac-mini-01, mac-pro-02, rack-07) drawn inside the dark rail at x=24. They are children of Topology and their coordinates are correct relative to it; the region itself is simply underneath Nav.

review_design reported no occlusion or overlap finding. overflow only checks a region against its parent's bounds, and a region that is fully inside the frame cannot overflow it — so this class of error is invisible to the current rules.

**Measured.** Topology (317:418) is placed at x=0 width=1120 and is fully occluded by the 220px nav rail for its first 220px; three device nodes are buried under it. No review rule covers region-vs-region occlusion.
